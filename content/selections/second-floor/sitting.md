### Configuration & Wiring

- **Speaker config:** 3.1 — Front L/R (BP-9020), Center (CS-9040), Sub (ProSub 600 or replacement).
- **No surrounds planned** at this stage; conduit rough-in (below) leaves the option open.
- **Powered components:** BP-9020 (×2), CS-9040, and ProSub 600 each need their own AC outlet at their final positions in addition to speaker-level / LFE signal.

### Rough-In Requirements (before drywall)

The exact speaker locations are TBD, so the strategy is **conduit, not hard-pulled cable** — let final placement get decided post-construction without opening walls.

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Electrical at TV wall | Standard duplex receptacle for the Denon AVR-S750H and TV (open console — no enclosure ventilation concerns) |
| Electrical at speaker locations | One 120V duplex receptacle near each powered speaker position (×2 BP-9020 + ×1 CS-9040 at TV wall + ×1 ProSub 600). Coordinate locations once furniture plan firms up |
| Network drop | One Cat6 to the AVR location for HEOS / AirPlay 2 / streaming services |
| Speaker/sub conduit | 1" smurf tube from AVR location (TV wall) to anticipated L tower, R tower, and sub positions — pull speaker cable + sub RCA later |
| Cable type (post-construction) | CL2/CL3-rated 14–16ga speaker wire for L/R/Center; shielded RCA / LFE cable for the sub |

> **ProSub 600 status:** Bob to bench-test before relying on it. If dead, the rough-in still stands — any modern ~$300–500 powered sub (e.g., SVS PB-1000, Klipsch R-100SW, Polk PSW10) will use the same single LFE RCA input + AC outlet.

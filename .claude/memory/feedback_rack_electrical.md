Every equipment rack (network, server, AV) gets a **dedicated 20A quad receptacle on its own circuit**. This is a standing rule, not a per-project decision.

**Why:** Isolates rack loads from general-purpose circuits (power tools, compressors, vacuums, kitchen appliances) that cause voltage sag and noise. Quad (vs duplex) provides spare outlets for service gear, test equipment, and future PDU additions without daisy-chaining. As a PE, Bob applies this consistently on his MEP designs.

**How to apply:**
- When specifying any rack location (residential or commercial), call out a dedicated 20A quad on its own circuit at the wall behind/under the rack.
- Do not propose sharing the rack circuit with adjacent receptacles, lighting, or HVAC.
- Mention this in rough-in scope items for contractors so framing/electrical accounts for the homerun.
- Applies to [[project_garage_av_rack]] AV cabinet and the utility room 42U rack in [[project_55murphy]].
